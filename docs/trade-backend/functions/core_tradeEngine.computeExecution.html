    • computeExecution(
          marketPrice: MarketPrice,
          request: BuyStockRequest,
          rng?: Rng,
      ): Execution

      Decides the executed price and whether the trade succeeds.

      Parameters

      • marketPrice: MarketPrice

        The symbol's price for the day.

      • request: BuyStockRequest

        The validated buy request.

      • rng: Rng = Math.random

        Random source; defaults to Math.random.

      Returns Execution

      The executed price and the success flag.

      Remarks

      Pure: no I/O, so a fixed rng gives a repeatable result. With range = highest minus lowest price:

      • market: a random price from lowest + 48% of the range up to highest; always succeeds.
      • agent_aggressive, agent_moderate, agent_conservative: a random price within lowest + 0-50%, 5-45% or 10-40% of the range; always succeed.
      • manual: a bid at or above highest executes at highest and succeeds; a bid at or below lowest is recorded at the bid and fails; a bid in between executes at the bid and succeeds only if a random point in the range lands above lowest + 45% of the range.

      Random prices are rounded to 2 decimals; the two manual edge cases return highest or the bid as given. Any other price_mode, including the legacy agent, falls through to the manual rules, so callers must validate the request first.
